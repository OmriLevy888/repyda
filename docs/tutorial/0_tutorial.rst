Welcome to Repyda' tutorial
===========================

Repyda' tutorial provides a comprehensive look at all of its functionalities.
It can be followed, part by part and by the end you will know its ins and outs.
You may also be interested in the :ref:`examples<general-examples>` for ways to
mix different parts of the library.

.. note::
    It is assumed you have already :ref:`installed repyda<general-install>`
